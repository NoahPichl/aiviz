Reflect on the experience with AI

For Phase I, we had to answer the question "How does the model behave?". As the AI-aided pair, GenerativeAI was an active part of our project and it can be seen in some of our plots and interpretations. 

We did some brainstorming with LLMs and I can say it got pretty overwhelming, pretty fast. We started with an idea and as we were "talking" and "talking" with the LLM, we got more ideas about which plots to use and which metrics to compare and it made us feel like everything can be done in a day. However, when we actually wanted to start the project, we did not know where to even start! There were so many implementations we could do, but some of them were showing the same result with a different visualisation. After analysing each part, we could see that most of the plots were "useless" or not enough to prove what we wanted to prove. 

This leads me to another problem, some LLMs take a specific route when solving a task and if you don't pay attention and investigate everything on your own, you will end up with a project that makes no sense or claims things in a strongly matter, as a fact, when there is not enough evidence to support the statement. This led to additional work, especially when dealing with code. We had to spend a lot of our time trying to understand what the LLM was doing and where it would modify our code. Sometimes the modifications were useless and led to even more frustration and work.

The claims that seem so valid can lead to a false sense of security, where you feel like everything can be done really easily and all you need to do is click or prompt something like “Please do the next part too” and the next and so on. Moreover, when you have the finished project, you will realise you have no actual knowledge of your projects. I do think that something like this might have happened with our team too. For some parts of the project, I do think the Human-only pair might have a better understanding than the AI-aided pair. Conversely, I also think the AI-aided pair managed to gain some extra information about the dataset and visualisation than the Human-only.  

Therefore, GenerativeAI can be a useful tool when used accordingly. However, everything needs to be taken with a grain of salt and you, as the human, have to make the actual connections between what works and what doesn’t.
